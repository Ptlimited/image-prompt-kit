# Style library

Paste a box into Nano Banana or ChatGPT. Use it as is, or as a starting point the skill can adapt.

## From your own photo

Attach 1 clear photo of yourself first. Tested on Nano Banana 2.1, and the results are in `examples/styles/`. ChatGPT takes the same prompts.

### 70s portrait

```
Turn the person in this photo into a 1970s film portrait. Keep their face exactly the same. Feathered hair, a brown suede jacket over a patterned shirt, aviator sunglasses. Warm faded film colors, soft grain, golden hour light on a street lined with palm trees. Shot on 35mm film.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Felted miniature

```
Turn the person in this photo into a tiny needle felted wool doll sitting at a little cafe table with a cup of coffee. Keep their hairstyle, glasses and outfit colors so it is clearly them. Fuzzy wool with visible fibers, rosy felt cheeks, photographed up close with a shallow depth of field and warm light.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Bobblehead

```
Turn the person in this photo into a collectible bobblehead figure with an oversized head and a small body, standing on a round base on a desk. Keep their face, hairstyle and glasses so it is clearly them. Glossy painted vinyl, a tiny name plate on the base that reads "THE BOSS", soft office light, and a blurred laptop and coffee mug behind it.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Newspaper front page

```
Put the person in this photo on the front page of an old fashioned newspaper called "The Daily Spark". Their portrait is a large black and white halftone photo. The giant headline reads "LOCAL LEGEND DOES IT AGAIN". Narrow columns of small print, a date line, yellowed newsprint, and a fold across the middle.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Marble statue

```
Turn the person in this photo into a white marble statue on a pedestal in a grand museum hall. Keep their face and hairstyle carved in exact detail. Flowing carved fabric, soft light from a glass ceiling, other statues and columns blurred in the background, and a small brass plaque on the pedestal.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

## Test prompts

The 5 prompts from the blind test in `test-results.md`. These work from text alone.

### Pencil sketch

```
A graphite pencil drawing of an old fisherman mending a net on a wooden dock at dawn. Loose sketchy lines at the edges, careful cross hatching on his weathered hands and face, the net spilling across his lap. Gulls are quick rough marks in the sky. Drawn on cream sketchbook paper with a visible grain, a soft smudge from the side of the hand, and faint construction lines left in.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Street photo

```
A candid street photo of a dumpling vendor at a night market in the rain. Steam rises from bamboo baskets under a red tarp, neon signs reflect in the puddles, and a customer holding an umbrella pays with their phone. Shot on a 35mm lens at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, slight motion blur on people walking past, natural skin texture, no retouching.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Over the top

```
An epic movie poster scene of a hamster in polished silver armor riding a corgi into battle across a giant breakfast table. Behind them, an army of toast soldiers charges past mountains of pancakes, syrup waterfalls, and a rising sun made of an egg yolk. Dramatic low angle, golden rim light, crumbs flying through the air, heroic and ridiculous at the same time.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Fantasy newspaper

```
The front page of an old fashioned newspaper called "The Morning Teacup", printed in black ink on yellowed newsprint. The giant headline reads "LOCAL CAT ELECTED MAYOR". The smaller headline below reads "Promises Naps For All, Fish On Fridays". A large black and white halftone photo shows a serious tabby cat in a tiny bow tie at a podium full of microphones. Narrow columns of small print, the date line, and a folded crease across the middle.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```

### Felted miniature scene

```
A needle felted wool miniature of a tiny barista bear making a latte in a little coffee shop. The bear wears a green apron and holds a small metal milk jug, with a heart drawn in the foam. Behind the counter, shelves hold tiny jars of beans and a chalkboard menu. Every surface is fuzzy wool with visible fibers, photographed up close with a shallow depth of field and warm cafe light.
Make it a vertical 4:5 image.
```
